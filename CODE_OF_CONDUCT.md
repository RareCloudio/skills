# Code of Conduct

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome advances, in any form.
- Assume good faith, and keep discussion on the work.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the private channel). Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation for conduct that breaks these rules.
